About Sinopia

Built on primary research, not secondary screening.

Sinopia is an independent strategic advisory practice working at the intersection of sanctions, designation practice and national-security policy. Engagements draw on active academic research into how blacklisting and proscription regimes are built, contested and misused — not on repackaged open-source screening.
